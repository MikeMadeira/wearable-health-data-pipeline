M-05 - UTC 'Z' timestamps in heart rate (P018, P044) HighImplemented

How it was detected
Timestamp-format scan per file: 2,880 heart-rate records in 2 participants end in Z; every other timestamp is naive. Each Z block is exactly one day. Read as plain labels, 600 records collide with existing minutes; shifted by -5 h all 2,880 land in empty slots with 0 collisions.
Human decision
Interpret naive timestamps as local UTC-5 and convert any Z/offset timestamp to that timeline before the local date is taken. Keep the raw text, mark it with the informational rule HR_TIMESTAMP_CONVERTED, no reject rows.
Why
A wrong clock shifts values 5 h (distorting time-of-day and sleep overlap) and creates a hole plus duplicates. The empty-slot fit is the evidence for the offset; the offset itself comes from the data dictionary and is an assumption (A-01).

Problem - as found

Hours 00-04 of 01-25 are empty and hours 00-04 of 01-26 hold twice the records: 300 collisions per participant.

M-07 - Duplicate minute slots (P010, P019, P022, P025 and others) HighImplemented

How it was detected
Group records by (participant, minute); compare values. P010, P019, P022 and P025 duplicate the whole of 2026-01-10 (and miss 2026-01-08) with conflicting values; P005, P034, P040 and P050 have a few duplicates each.
Human decision
Preserve all raw rows. Exact duplicates count once. Conflicting values at one minute: withhold that measure for the minute (no first/last pick, no average). Unrelated slots and measures stay usable.
Why
Choosing a value would invent data; averaging unrelated readings hides the conflict. A-04 (a mis-stamped day) was suspected but the values do not match, so equivalence is not proven.

Caveat: silver is not de-duplicated. Aggregate gold, or de-duplicate by date_trunc('minute', minute_ts). Heavy conflict days keep few valid minutes, so always read *_valid_minute_count next to a total.

M-01 - Undocumented _unit = steps_per_hour (P005, P033, P038) HighImplemented

How it was detected
Schema scan: an extra _unit field in 120,963 step records of 3 participants. Their medians are about 60x the others (P033 300, P038 420 vs 5). Every such value is a non-negative multiple of 60.
Human decision
Convert per record: normalized = steps / 60 when the unit is steps_per_hour and the value divisible by 60; otherwise withhold. Raw value and unit text stay in silver. Decided per record, never per participant. (First withheld, then reversed on 2026-10-13.)
Why
Converted medians (1, 5, 7) are comparable to the 47 other participants (5), so converting beats discarding 3 participants. The unit meaning is supported by the data but not confirmed by the data provider (A-02).

M-02 / M-03 - Impossible heart rates and extreme steps (P029, P037) HighImplemented

How it was detected
Range checks against the dictionary: 4 heart-rate values outside 40-200 (-10 and 999), 2 negative step counts, and 95,902 step records above 30 per minute (up to 1,000,000).
Human decision
Heart rate outside 40-200: keep raw, withhold the normalized value, exclude from aggregates. Negative steps: withhold. Steps above 30/min: not rejected, classified as the burst level and kept in totals; no positive upper cutoff is approved.
Why
A 999 bpm reading would corrupt a day's min/max/mean, while high step bursts can be genuine, so the rule removes only what is physically impossible. Only the affected field is lost; the same row's other fields survive.

M-04 - Stuck heart-rate sensor (P003, P042) MediumImplemented

How it was detected
Run-length scan of identical consecutive in-range values: exactly two runs of 180 minutes (P003 from 2026-01-09 04:44, P042 from 2026-01-24 09:16); no other run reaches even 10 minutes, so any threshold between 10 and 180 gives the same result.
Human decision
Flag every minute of a run of at least 60 identical consecutive minute slots, from its first minute; keep the raw reading, withhold it from aggregates. A missing minute, out-of-range value or conflict breaks a run.
Why
In-range values pass the range check, so only a temporal rule can catch a flat line. The 60-minute threshold is provisional (no validated device behaviour) and is not sensitive on this dataset.

M-08 / X-01 - Gaps on the minute grid HighImplemented

How it was detected
Expected 1,440 slots per participant-day minus observed slots: steps miss 6,840 slots in 7 participants (whole day 2026-01-08 for P010, P019, P022, P025; 360 minutes for P020, P028, P041); heart rate misses 9,720 before and 6,840 after Z conversion.

No imputation. Aggregate observed eligible readings and expose *_valid_minute_count and *_present; missing steps are not zero and missing heart rate is not a measured value.

S-01..S-07 - sleep durations, efficiency and the meaning of sleep_end MediumImplemented

How it was detected
Compare stage sums, stage timestamps and the session span: the last stage is awake and starts at sleep_end in 1,397 of 1,400 sessions, so sleep_end is the wake-up, not the end of the last stage. The sum of all stages differs from the span in all 1,400 sessions; the non-awake sum in 7. The supplied efficiency is exactly 100 in 1,132 sessions and matches the stage-based value within 1 point in only 7. 4 stages disagree with their timestamps.
Human decision
total_sleep_min = non-awake duration_min; efficiency = non-awake / all stage minutes (denominator is the stage timeline, not sleep_end/onset); duration_min is used and mismatches flagged with both values kept; the awake tail is not sleep; day = wake date.
Why
A span denominator gives ~100% for almost every night (meaningless), while the stage-based value spans 74.6-94.4 (median 87.4). Wake-date assignment is 1:1 with the survey; onset-date assignment would put 43 sessions outside the window. 'sleep_end is the start of the awake tail' is an inference (A-03).

T-02 / T-03 - Blank firmware_version and the suspicious 2.1.8 MediumDecided - NOT yet implemented

How it was detected
Blank check on device_metadata.csv: 15 of 50 rows have no firmware_version, but device_label carries a parseable fw<version> in all 15 with 0 conflicts where both exist. All 15 parse to 2.1.8, a value in no populated cell (populated: 2.2.0 x23, 2.2.1 x12).
Human decision
Parse device_label when the structured field is blank, keep the raw blank, record fallback provenance, flag 2.1.8 for review, never discard the device row.
Why
The label is reliable where both exist, but the blanks all being one older version suggests missing-not-at-random, so the value is trusted only with provenance and a flag.

Honest status: device_metadata only reaches raw_manifest; no firmware column or parsing exists in the pipeline, and gold_participant_devices is not produced. The right-hand view is a preview, not pipeline output.
